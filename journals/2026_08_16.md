- #coffee/dial-in #coffee/pourover [Throw Out Your Pour Over Recipe - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BbDshsxJDKo) [[Daddy Got Coffee/Throw Out Your Pour Over Recipe]]
	- Slow, small circles always
	- Draw down needs to be between 3 to 4 minutes. 1m 30s is less. This is as much a useful feedback as taste.
	- Bloom can be shorter when not much gas escaping
	- Grind size as a lever for body/texture when finer and clear/bright when coarser.
	- Ratio between 14 to 18 can work.
		- Higher give lighter, cleaner, nuanced cups. Especially with hard-to-extract beans.
		- Lower gives body and intensity.
	- Temperatures between 85 to 98
		- Too hot and fruity notes are driven off.
			- **If coffee smells great when brewing, but nothing in cup, drop the temp!**
	- Pour and agitation
		- More pours give more body and less clarity
		- Fewer pours higher clarity
	- Brewer geometry
		- Flat-bottom
			- Forgiving, more body, less clarity
	- Filter paper, if fast or slow
	- Higher altitude, denser coffees, hard to extract.
	- Couple of weeks for off-gassing/resting.
