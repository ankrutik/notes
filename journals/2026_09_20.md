- #reading/manifesto [[Reading Book Stand]]
	- I'm currently using it on the window. Natural sunlight on it, the stand board turned at an angle toward me such that the light bounces off revealing the texture of the paper's grain.
	- #reading/annotations
		- Stick a post-it on a bookmark. Split in middle with a horizontal line.
		- Basic annotation themes of
			- Prose
				- Strictly for flagging good writing
			- Excerpta/General/Miscellanea
				- Anything worth flagging without a theme
		- Imagine a page visually divided in 5 parts. When marking annotation, use the page number and division in that order as follows...
			- 126.3, 320.5
	- Keep the post-it below the reading stand ready for annotation
