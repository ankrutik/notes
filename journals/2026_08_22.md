- #coffee/pourover [[What is working currently with.../V60]]
	- ## Grind
		- Coarser grind on the Comandante > 45 Red Clicks
		- Start with 50, then go up or down as per [[Daddy Got Coffee/Throw Out Your Pour Over Recipe]]
	- ## Pour
		- Circle, not spiral pours
		- Pre-heat the gooseneck by pouring out water for a few seconds, feel the gooseneck get warm
	- ## Ratio
		- Stick to bloom ratio of 3
		- Start with a 15 ratio, go up or down as per [[Daddy Got Coffee/Throw Out Your Pour Over Recipe]]
			- Start with 3 pour
				- bloom = weight x 3
				- brew = total ratio - bloom
				- each pour = brew/2
			- If harsh in flavour, consider either of following after bloom
				- 2 pour with last pour in center
					- end center pour  with 1 or 2 full circles
				- 1 pour in circle
	- ## Temperatures
		- Temperature shifts by 2-3 degrees
		- **If you're sensing fragrance when brewing but not in cup, reduce temperature**
		- Natural processing: 88
		- Washed: 92
	- ## Paper
		- start with ABACA
		- T90 or Origami Cone if you want less contact time
		- T92 or T83 if you want more contact time
