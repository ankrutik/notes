- #coffee/pourover
	- All grind size conversions from Comandante MK4 to Timemore C2 done at https://brewmark.io/tools/grind-converter
	- **Recipes**
		- Bloom is 3 times coffee weight
		- Bloom is thin spiral pour
		- All pours other than bloom are thin circle pours (not spiral) in mid section of the brewer. Draw circles very slowly.
		- Before initiating all pours, pour a thin stream into another glass for 3-5 seconds to ensure gooseneck of your kettle is not loosing the water's heat.
		- Divide the rest of the water into 2 or 3
		- 15gm coffee
		- 225gm water for 1:15 ratio
		- Use ABACA Plus filter where possible
		- **2 pour or "one-and-done"**
			- 0' : pour bloom, 45gm
			- 45'' : pour to 225gm
		- **3 pour or "standard"**
			- 0' : pour bloom, 45gm
			- 45'' : pour to 135gm
			- ~1'25'' or after draw down from surface : pour to 225gm
