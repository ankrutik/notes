- #DnD
	- Dice notation
		- 3d8 + 5 means take a d8 dice, roll it 3 times, add results, then add 5
		- 1d100 uses 2 d10 dice.
			- You designate one of the dice to be tens digit and the other ones.
			- 7 and 1 gives 71
			- 0 and 0 gives 100
	- **D20 Test** to see if character will succeed or fail at an action
	- **Damage**, after a attack D20 test, you roll again to determine how much damage was caused
	- **Random tables**, use a table and die roll for a random result
	- **Percentile dice**, if for example there is 5% chance of a thing happening, die roll should be less than or equal to 5 for that to happen.
