	- Sales Funnel #mental-models
		- a "sales funnel" is wide at the top and very narrow at the bottom. Lots of outreach yields small number of responses of which even smaller number will do what you want them to do of which a tiny number will actually "close". Applying for jobs is similar.
		- Engineers are used to predictive/known outcomes, need repetition to get used to this funnel, for emotional calluses to develop.
		- Consider temporary paid software tools like premium features in LinkedIn.
