- #movegraphs #tech/microservices
	- Always design microservices by cost. Keep cost low and your service will survive management changes and whims.
	- Have architect approve if services need to be microservices or not, not the devs.
		- Architect to be aware of the eventual cost leeching nature of platforms like AWS
		- Knowledge transfer of this motivation and guard rails when architect leaves
	- Common codebase, split microservices at deployment level
		- Use load balancer or redirection for microservices
		- If cost increases, you just have to remove redirection and delete the many redundant deployments
	- The management will change, the funding will change, their viewpoint will change. Design your codebase to weather these changes quickly and with minimum impact.
